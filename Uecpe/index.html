WHAT IS COMPUTER ENGINEERING?

Computer Engineering combines the principles of hardware and software to design, develop, and improve modern computer systems and intelligent technologies. At the University of the East – Manila, Computer Engineering students develop knowledge and practical skills in computer hardware, programming, networking, embedded systems, electronics, and emerging technologies.
