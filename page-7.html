What is SCPES?

SCPES is the student organization for Computer Engineering students, focused on leadership, service, and academic development.

What do the officers do?

Student officers organize events, represent the program, and support student engagement in technology and campus activities.

Why is SCPES important?

It helps students build confidence, leadership, teamwork, and a stronger sense of community beyond their classes.
