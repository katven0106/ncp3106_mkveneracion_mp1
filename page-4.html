What is Computer Engineering?

Computer Engineering combines electronics, computer hardware, software, and systems design.

What subjects are part of the program?

The program includes programming, digital logic, electronics, networking, architecture, and systems integration.

Is programming required?

Programming is an important part of the program and supports work with hardware, applications, and intelligent systems.

Does the program include hardware?

Yes. Students study circuits, processors, microcontrollers, sensors, and computer architecture.

What technologies can students explore?

Students may explore embedded systems, IoT, robotics, cloud platforms, cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence.

Are laboratory activities included?

Laboratory activities help students apply engineering concepts through experiments, testing, and project work.

What skills does the program develop?

The program develops analytical thinking, technical problem-solving, communication, teamwork, and design skills.

What careers are available after graduation?

Graduates may work as computer, software, hardware, network, cybersecurity, cloud, data, or robotics engineers.

Can graduates work in research?

Yes. Computer Engineering graduates can pursue research, development, prototyping, and graduate studies.

Where can more information be requested?

Program information may be requested through the University of the East Manila and the Computer Engineering department.

Computer Engineering Specializations

Computer Engineering connects hardware, software, and systems thinking. These fields lead to useful products, secure infrastructure, intelligent machines, and careers with real-world impact.

Innovation in action

Explore the fields shaping tomorrow

Discover how computer engineering turns ideas into connected systems, intelligent machines, secure platforms, and useful digital experiences.

01

Embedded Systems

Design computer systems inside everyday products, combining microcontrollers, sensors, firmware, and electronics.

Tools and careers: Arduino, STM32, real-time operating systems, smart appliances, embedded systems engineer.

02

Internet of Things

Connect physical devices so they can collect data, communicate, and respond intelligently to their surroundings.

Tools and careers: MQTT, Raspberry Pi, smart homes, connected agriculture, IoT solutions developer.

03

Computer Networks

Build and manage the systems that move information reliably between computers, devices, and users.

Tools and careers: TCP/IP, Cisco networking, data centers, enterprise networks, network administrator.

04

Cybersecurity

Protect data, devices, applications, and networks by finding weaknesses and designing stronger defenses.

Tools and careers: ethical hacking, firewalls, SIEM, incident response, security analyst.

05

Software Development

Turn ideas into dependable applications through programming, user-focused design, testing, and maintenance.

Tools and careers: Python, JavaScript, Git, web and mobile apps, software engineer.

06

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

Teach computers to recognize patterns, make predictions, and support decisions using data and algorithms.

Tools and careers: Python, TensorFlow, computer vision, chatbots, machine learning engineer.

07

Data Science and Data Engineering

Collect, organize, analyze, and explain data so organizations can make informed technical and business decisions.

Tools and careers: SQL, Python, Power BI, data pipelines, data engineer.

08

Robotics and Automation

Combine mechanics, electronics, programming, and control systems to make machines perform useful tasks.

Tools and careers: ROS, robotic arms, autonomous vehicles, factory automation, robotics engineer.

09

Computer Hardware and Architecture

Understand and improve the processors, memory, circuits, and architecture that make computing possible.

Tools and careers: FPGA, Verilog, digital logic, computer systems, hardware designer.

10

Cloud and Edge Computing

Place computing power where it is most useful, from scalable cloud platforms to fast local edge devices.

Tools and careers: AWS, Azure, Docker, edge analytics, cloud solutions architect.
