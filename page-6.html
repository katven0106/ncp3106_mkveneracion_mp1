Who are the faculty members?

The Computer Engineering faculty includes mentors, coordinators, and specialists who guide students in engineering, design, and research.

What do faculty members do?

They teach courses, supervise projects, mentor students, and support academic growth in engineering and technology.

Are faculty members accessible to students?

Yes. Faculty members are available for guidance, consultations, and academic support throughout the program.

Do faculty members help in research?

Many faculty members support research, innovation, and applied technology projects that connect theory with practice.
